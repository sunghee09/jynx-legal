Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Jynx ("we", "us") is a social discovery app that connects people through shared real-time activities. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what control you have. Jynx is available to users aged 18 and over only.

1. Information You Give Us

  • Account details — name, email address, and authentication identifier provided by Google or Apple when you sign in. We never see or store your Google or Apple password.
  • Date of birth — used to confirm you are 18 or older.
  • Profile information — the photo you upload for your avatar, your display name, bio, gender, and country.
  • Onboarding responses — personality type (MBTI) or vibe slider answers, selected interests, and character identity selections. Some of these are private preference signals used for matching and are never displayed on your public profile.
  • Content you post — activity status, captions, photos, and any place, music, or event details you choose to attach to a post.
  • Direct messages — the content of messages you send to other users.

2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Location — if you grant permission, we may collect your approximate or precise device location to show you nearby activity and to attach a place to your posts. You can decline this permission or revoke it at any time in your device settings; Jynx remains usable without it. We do not continuously track your location in the background.
  • Device and usage data — device model, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and general usage patterns, collected through Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Timestamps — when posts, messages, and activity updates are created, so the app can show what is recent.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To show your posts to other users and show you theirs.
  • To match you with people doing similar activities or sharing interests.
  • To deliver direct messages between users.
  • To generate your concealed-face avatar.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent abuse, harassment, and violations of our Terms.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve app stability.

4. Your Avatar and Photo Processing

Jynx requires every user to use an avatar in which the face is artistically concealed. When you create an avatar, the photo you upload is processed to detect and conceal facial features. This processing uses Stability AI, which means your uploaded image is transmitted to and processed by Stability AI's servers for this purpose. The resulting avatar is stored in our Firebase Storage. We do not use your photos to train any machine learning model of our own.

5. Third-Party Services

We share data with the following providers strictly to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging) — hosts your account, posts, messages, and images. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics — usage analytics and crash reporting.
  • Stability AI — image processing for avatar creation. See Stability AI's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with advertisers.

6. What Other Users Can See

  • Your display name, bio, avatar, activity posts, and any place, music, or event context you attach to them.
  • Your follower and following counts are visible only to you. They are never shown on your profile to anyone else.
  • Your private matching preferences and character identity selections are never displayed publicly.
  • Direct messages are visible only to you and the person you are messaging.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. Posts in the RSVP tab expire automatically after their associated date passes. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, posts, and uploaded images. Messages you have sent may remain visible to their recipients. Backup copies and records required for legal, security, or abuse-prevention purposes may be retained for a limited period after deletion.

8. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and correction — you can view and edit your profile information in the app at any time.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account from Settings. This action cannot be undone.
  • Location — you can grant or revoke location permission in your device settings.
  • Notifications — you can turn off notifications in the app or your device settings.
  • Blocking — you can block any user, which prevents them from seeing your posts or contacting you.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, or port your data, and the right to object to processing. If you are a California resident, you have rights under the CCPA including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to request its deletion. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

9. International Data Transfers

Jynx is operated from the Republic of Korea, and our infrastructure is hosted by Google Firebase in the United States. If you use Jynx from outside these countries, your information will be transferred to and processed in them. We rely on standard contractual clauses and our providers' data protection commitments to safeguard these transfers.

10. Security

We use database and storage security rules that restrict access to your data, encryption in transit, and authentication through Google and Apple. Direct messages are readable only by the two participants in a conversation. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children

Jynx is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected information from a person under 18, we will delete that account and its data. If you believe a minor is using Jynx, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app develops. We will revise the date at the top and, for significant changes, notify you within the app.

13. Contact
