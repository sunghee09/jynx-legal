Delete Your MiRRA Account

Last updated: November 8, 2026

You can delete your MiRRA account and its data at any time. Deleting it cannot be undone.

If you have the app

  1. Open MiRRA and go to your Profile tab.
  2. Tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

If you no longer have the app

Email mirra.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my MiRRA account". Send it from the email address you used to sign in to MiRRA (your Google or Apple email), so we can tell the account is yours. If you used Apple's "Hide My Email", tell us that and we will help you prove the account is yours.

We reply to confirm, and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos and avatar.
  • Your posts and their photos.
  • Your chats, for the other person too.
  • Your bookmarks, private interests, keychain, crews you joined, and the rest of your account data.

What may be kept for a while

  • A conversation that someone reported, kept for 90 days (longer if the account was banned or the police lawfully ask), together with the reported account's email, phone number and sign-in.
  • A scrambled code of a phone number or a phone, kept so that a banned account cannot sign up again.
  • Backup copies, for a limited time.

The full details are in section 8 of our Privacy Policy.
